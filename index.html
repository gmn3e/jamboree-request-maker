Use it like an app Add it to your home screen for one-tap access
  1. In Safari, tap the Page Menu button in the address bar, then tap Share. If your tabs are set to the Top or Bottom layout, tap Share directly.

  3. Make sure Open as Web App is turned on, then tap Add.
  1. In Chrome, tap the ⋮ menu at the top right.
